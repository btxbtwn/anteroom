- When Mercari says an item is no longer available, Delist now records that listing as already gone instead of failing. It is never counted as a sale.
- After a sale, a delist that didn't go through is tried again automatically for eligible sales from the last two weeks, when Delist the other marketplaces is on. eBay and Etsy sales on the web app now start that delist too.
- A red alert in the sidebar lists sold-out items from the last two weeks that are still listed on another marketplace, and the sale notice on the item says why each delist failed.
