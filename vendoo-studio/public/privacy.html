Anteroom Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Anteroom helps resellers write listings and cross-list them to eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop and Etsy. This policy covers the Anteroom app, the website at myanteroom.com, and the Anteroom Bridge Chrome extension.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and sign-in details.
  • Your listings: the photos, titles, descriptions, prices and other listing details you create or import.
  • Marketplace connections: sign-in tokens for the marketplaces you connect, so Anteroom can create and update listings on your behalf. These are stored encrypted and are never shown back to any app or page.

What the Chrome extension does

Anteroom Bridge acts only on marketplace sites you are signed in to (eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Etsy and Vendoo), and only when you ask Anteroom to import, save, list, update or delist something. To do that it:

  • reads your sign-in session on those sites, so requests run as you;
  • opens and fills those sites' listing forms and reads the results;
  • sends listing details and results back to your own Anteroom, either the app on your Mac or your myanteroom.com account.

The extension does not read other websites, track your browsing, or show ads. Nothing is published or delisted unless you confirm it in Anteroom.

How we use it

Only to run Anteroom for you: generating listing text, keeping your inventory in sync, and carrying out the marketplace actions you request. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the services that run Anteroom (hosting, database and storage providers, and the AI provider that writes listing text from your photos).

Where it is stored

On the Mac app, your data stays on your Mac, and secrets stay in the macOS Keychain. For a myanteroom.com account, data is stored with Supabase and Fly.io in the United States, and each seller's data is kept separate from every other seller's.

Your choices

You can delete listings at any time, disconnect a marketplace, or uninstall the extension. To delete your account and its data, email us and we will remove it within 30 days.
