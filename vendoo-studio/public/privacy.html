Anteroom Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Anteroom helps resellers write listings and cross-list them to eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop and Etsy. This policy covers the Anteroom app, the website at myanteroom.com, and the Anteroom Bridge Chrome extension.

What we collect

  • Your Anteroom account: your email address and sign-in details.
  • Your listings: the photos, titles, descriptions, prices and other listing details you create, or import from your marketplace and Vendoo accounts.
  • Your marketplace accounts: whether you are signed in to each marketplace and the account name it shows (your username or display name, or the email address on the account when the marketplace gives no name). For eBay and Etsy, which Anteroom connects to directly, the sign-in tokens you approve, stored encrypted and never shown back to any app or page.
  • Your sales: for each order on your marketplace accounts, the order number, status and date, and the amounts: item price, shipping, refunds, marketplace fees and your shipping cost. Anteroom does not collect buyers' names, addresses or messages, and never sees card or bank details.
  • Interest in your listings: which of your listings were liked and when, the like count, and how many people an offer would reach. Not who liked them.
  • Vendoo: if you use Vendoo, your Vendoo account ID and email address, and the Vendoo drafts Anteroom creates or reads for you.
  • Sold prices for sourcing (Mac app): prices of sold items from eBay's public sold-search results, to price items you are considering buying.

What the Chrome extension does

Anteroom Bridge works on the marketplace sites you are signed in to in Chrome (Poshmark, Mercari, Depop and eBay), Vendoo, and Anteroom itself. It uses your existing sign-in on those sites so requests run as you. Your marketplace cookies and sign-in tokens stay in Chrome: the extension uses them there and does not send them to Anteroom.

When you ask, it:

  • fills a marketplace's listing form to save a draft, list, or update a listing, and delists a listing, only after you confirm that action in Anteroom;
  • imports your existing listings and your sales history;
  • creates and reads drafts in your Vendoo account;
  • checks which marketplaces you are signed in to, and opens a sign-in page.

Automatically, while Chrome is connected to Anteroom (the Mac app is running, or you have myanteroom.com open):

  • Sale detection (on unless you turn it off): every few minutes it reads your recent orders on Poshmark, Mercari and Depop, so Anteroom can mark sold items. Turn it off for each marketplace in Settings → Connections.
  • Delist after a sale (off unless you turn it on): when an item sells, it delists that item on your other marketplaces. Turn it on or off with Delist the other marketplaces in Settings → Connections.
  • Shares and offers (off unless you turn them on): shares your Poshmark closet at the times you choose, and sends offers to people who liked your listings on Poshmark, Mercari and Depop, never below the minimum price you set. To know when to offer, it checks your listings for new likes every minute. You turn each rule on, and off again, in Automations.
  • Sold prices (Mac app): when Sourcing refreshes, it opens eBay's public sold-search results in a background tab and reads the prices.

The extension never creates or publishes a listing on its own. It does not read other websites, record your browsing history, collect analytics, or show ads.

How we use it

Only to run Anteroom for you: writing listing text, keeping your inventory and sales in sync, and carrying out the marketplace actions you request or turn on. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or share it with anyone except the services that run Anteroom (hosting, database and storage providers, and the AI provider that writes listing text from your photos).

Where it is stored

On the Mac app, your data stays on your Mac, and secrets stay in the macOS Keychain. For a myanteroom.com account, data is stored with Supabase and Fly.io in the United States, and each seller's data is kept separate from every other seller's.

Your choices

You can turn off sale detection, delisting after a sale, and every share or offer rule at any time. You can delete listings, disconnect a marketplace, or uninstall the extension. To delete your account and its data, email us and we will remove it within 30 days.
