Anteroom Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

Anteroom helps resellers write listings and cross-list them to eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop and Etsy. This policy covers the Anteroom website at myanteroom.com, the Anteroom iOS and macOS apps, the Anteroom Bridge Chrome extension, and the Anteroom webhook relay.

What we collect

  • Your Anteroom account: your email address and sign-in details.
  • Your listings: the photos, titles, descriptions, prices and other listing details you create, or import from your marketplace and Vendoo accounts.
  • Your marketplace accounts: whether you are signed in to each marketplace and the account name it shows (your username or display name, or the email address on the account when the marketplace gives no name). For eBay and Etsy, which Anteroom connects to directly, the sign-in tokens you approve, stored encrypted and never shown back to any app or page. If you choose to turn on Poshmark Cloud for your hosted account, your Poshmark session cookies are also stored encrypted in Supabase Vault (isolated per seller and never shown back to any app or page) so Anteroom can run your Poshmark actions, sale checks, and automations when Chrome is closed; you can disconnect and delete those stored cookies at any time in Settings → Connections.
  • Your sales: for each order on your marketplace accounts, the order number, status and date, and the amounts: item price, shipping, refunds, marketplace fees and your shipping cost. Anteroom does not collect buyers' names, addresses or messages, and never sees card or bank details.
  • Interest in your listings: which of your listings were liked and when, the like count, and how many people an offer would reach. Not who liked them.
  • Vendoo: if you use Vendoo, your Vendoo account ID and email address, and the Vendoo drafts Anteroom creates or reads for you.
  • Sold prices for sourcing: prices of sold items from eBay's public sold-search results, to price items and wholesale lots you are considering buying.
  • Error diagnostics: sanitized server and browser error reports (such as error types, route names, and stack traces without listing text, photos, secrets, or buyer information) used solely to keep the service reliable and fix bugs.

What the Chrome extension does

Anteroom Bridge works on the marketplace sites you are signed in to in Chrome (Poshmark, Mercari, Depop and eBay), Vendoo, and Anteroom itself. It uses your existing sign-in on those sites so requests run as you. By default, your marketplace cookies and sign-in tokens stay in Chrome and are never sent to Anteroom. If you explicitly choose Connect in Cloud for Poshmark on myanteroom.com and confirm in the extension's approval window, the extension sends only your Poshmark cookies directly to Anteroom over HTTPS using a single-use upload token so they can be stored encrypted for your hosted account.

When you ask, it:

  • fills a marketplace's listing form to save a draft, list, or update a listing, and delists a listing, only after you confirm that action in Anteroom;
  • imports your existing listings and your sales history;
  • creates and reads drafts in your Vendoo account;
  • checks which marketplaces you are signed in to, opens a sign-in page, or (when you explicitly confirm Connect in Cloud for Poshmark) uploads your Poshmark session directly to your encrypted cloud vault.

Automatically, while Chrome is connected to Anteroom (the desktop app is running, or you have myanteroom.com open):

  • Sale detection (on unless you turn it off): every few minutes it reads your recent orders on Poshmark, Mercari and Depop, so Anteroom can mark sold items. Turn it off for each marketplace in Settings → Connections.
  • Delist after a sale (off unless you turn it on): when an item sells, it delists that item on your other marketplaces, and for two weeks after the sale tries again until each marketplace confirms the delist. It only retries the listings the sale left up, never one put up after the sale. Turn it on or off with Delist the other marketplaces in Settings → Connections.
  • Shares and offers (off unless you turn them on): shares your Poshmark closet at the times you choose, and sends offers to people who liked your listings on Poshmark, Mercari and Depop, never below the minimum price you set. To know when to offer, it checks your listings for new likes every minute. You turn each rule on, and off again, in Automations.
  • Sold prices (desktop app): when Sourcing refreshes, it opens eBay's public sold-search results in a background tab and reads the prices.

The extension never creates or publishes a listing on its own. It does not read other websites, record your browsing history, collect analytics, or show ads.

How we use it

Only to run Anteroom for you: writing listing text, keeping your inventory and sales in sync, diagnosing failures, and carrying out the marketplace actions you request or turn on. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or share it with third parties except the service providers listed below.

Service providers

To operate the hosted service, Anteroom uses the following infrastructure and AI providers:

  • Supabase (United States): authentication, Postgres database, and private encrypted photo storage for myanteroom.com and iPhone accounts.
  • Fly.io (United States): hosted application servers and background workers for myanteroom.com.
  • Cloudflare: Workers webhook relay for eBay and Etsy OAuth callbacks, sale notifications, and public eBay research lookups.
  • AI model providers (OpenRouter, or the AI provider you connect in Settings → Providers): generates listing text, reads measurements and tags from your photos, and powers the business assistant.

Where it is stored

On the macOS app in local mode, your data stays on your Mac and secrets stay in the macOS Keychain. For a myanteroom.com or iPhone account, data is stored with Supabase and Fly.io in the United States, and each seller's data is isolated from every other seller's.

Your choices and account deletion

You can turn off sale detection, delisting after a sale, and every share or offer rule at any time. You can delete listings, disconnect a marketplace, or uninstall the extension.

You can permanently delete your hosted account and all Anteroom data immediately from Account → Delete account… on the web or Settings → Account → Delete account… on iPhone. Deleting your account removes your listings, photos, sales records, automations, assistant chats, settings, AI keys, and marketplace sign-ins from Anteroom; listings that are already live on marketplaces stay there. You can also email us for assistance with privacy or deletion requests.
